ABOUT US

About Sathwika Foundation

A compassionate initiative focused on supporting people through education, food assistance, and community care.

OUR STORY

Serving with purpose and care

Sathwika Foundation is a compassionate initiative focused on supporting people through education, food assistance, and community care. Established on October 28, 2025, the foundation was created with a vision to serve and uplift those in need.

Our Beginning

Started as an individual initiative with deep personal significance, Sathwika Foundation began its journey by providing food to more than 500 people on its very first day. This marked a strong commitment to kindness, service, and social responsibility.

Our Mission

Our mission is to help every individual in need by ensuring access to basic necessities and fostering a sense of care within communities.

Our Vision

We aim to build a better future where support, dignity, and opportunity are accessible to all.

Our Commitment

Through education, food assistance, and community care, we strive to create meaningful support for people and encourage a culture of kindness and social responsibility.

WHAT GUIDES US

Our Core Values

Our work is guided by simple values that inspire us to serve with sincerity and responsibility.

Compassion

We believe in caring for people with kindness and empathy.

Service

We strive to support those in need through meaningful community action.

Community

We believe positive change grows stronger when communities come together.
