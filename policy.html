TRUST & TRANSPARENCY

Policies & Terms

Our commitment to privacy, responsibility, transparency, and respectful community service.

OUR PRINCIPLES

Built on trust and responsibility

Sathwika Foundation aims to maintain a safe, respectful, and transparent environment for everyone who interacts with our initiative. These policies explain how we handle information and how volunteers, contributors, and blood donors can participate.

Privacy Policy

At Sathwika Foundation, we respect and protect the privacy of individuals who interact with us. Any personal information voluntarily shared with us is handled responsibly and used only for legitimate purposes connected to our activities.

Information such as names, phone numbers, email addresses, or other contact details may be used for communication, coordination, record-keeping, and providing appropriate support.

We do not intentionally sell or share personal information for commercial purposes. Information may only be disclosed when necessary to provide requested support, with appropriate consent, or when required by applicable law.

By voluntarily providing information through our website or communication channels, you acknowledge that the information may be used for the purposes described in this policy.

Blood Donor Details & Privacy

Individuals who wish to become blood donors may voluntarily register through the Blood Donation page of the Sathwika Foundation website.

During donor registration, we may collect limited information that is necessary to help coordinate potential blood donation support.

Information We May Collect

  • Donor's Name
  • Phone Number
  • Blood Group
  • General Location

The information provided by a potential donor is intended to help us identify and communicate with suitable donors when there is a blood-related request or emergency situation.

Donor information should be provided voluntarily and accurately. Donors should not provide information belonging to another person without their permission.

We aim to handle donor information responsibly and use it only for purposes related to blood donation coordination, communication, and relevant support.

Sathwika Foundation does not guarantee that every registered donor will be contacted, selected, or able to donate blood. Actual blood donation eligibility and suitability are determined by the donor and the relevant medical or blood bank professionals.

Important Information

Blood donor registration through our website is only an expression of willingness to help. It does not constitute a medical assessment, blood donation guarantee, or emergency medical service. In an emergency, individuals should contact the appropriate hospital, blood bank, or emergency service without delay.

Data Security & Retention

We take reasonable steps to protect personal information shared with us from unauthorized access, misuse, alteration, or disclosure.

Personal information may be retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for communication, coordination, record-keeping, or legitimate operational purposes.

While we make reasonable efforts to protect information, no method of electronic transmission or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Contributions & Financial Transparency

Sathwika Foundation currently does not collect monetary donations from the public through this website.

Individuals who wish to contribute to society are welcome to support our mission by participating as volunteers or by offering appropriate non-monetary assistance.

Individuals interested in contributing their time, skills, or resources are welcome to contact us directly so that suitable ways to participate can be discussed.

Any future changes to our contribution or fundraising activities will be communicated transparently and carried out in accordance with applicable laws and regulations.

Volunteer Terms

Volunteers at Sathwika Foundation play an important role in supporting our mission. By participating, volunteers agree to act responsibly, respectfully, and in alignment with the values of the Foundation.

Volunteers are expected to maintain appropriate conduct, respect the dignity and privacy of beneficiaries, and cooperate with other members during activities.

Sathwika Foundation reserves the right to discontinue volunteer participation if an individual's conduct is found to be inappropriate, unsafe, unlawful, or inconsistent with the Foundation's principles.

Community Responsibility

Sathwika Foundation aims to treat every individual with dignity, respect, and fairness. We do not support discrimination, harassment, abuse, or any form of harmful conduct.

Everyone participating in our activities is expected to contribute towards maintaining a safe and respectful environment for the community.

Have Questions About Our Policies?

If you have any questions, concerns, or require clarification about our policies, please feel free to contact Sathwika Foundation.
